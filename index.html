Conspiracy Detective

Work out who’s innocent. Catch the culprit.

How to play

  1. Every case has 5 suspects and exactly one culprit.
  2. You start with 2 clues. Each clue proves one innocent suspect couldn’t have done it, but it never says who. You have to work it out.
  3. Tap “Rule out” to cross people off as you go (just your notes). Tap “Accuse” when you’re ready.
  4. Right with 2 clues = 3 points. Reveal a 3rd clue and it’s worth 2, a 4th clue and it’s worth 1. Wrong = 0.

Example case

Who ate the last slice of pizza?

Suspects: The dog · The roommate · The pizza guy · Grandma · The neighbor

Clue 1: “No paw prints and no drool anywhere near the kitchen.”Dogs leave those. So it wasn’t The dog.
Clue 2: “The delivery receipt shows he left at 8:05pm, and the pizza was still full at 9.”So it wasn’t The pizza guy.

You start with these 2 clues. Three suspects are left: The roommate, Grandma, The neighbor. You could guess now for 3 points, or reveal more:

Clue 3: “Her cruise ship didn’t dock until this morning.”It wasn’t Grandma. Now 2 are left (worth 2 points).
Clue 4: “He was on a video call with 40 coworkers all evening.”It wasn’t The neighbor. Only The roommate is left (worth 1 point).
